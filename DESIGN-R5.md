# Token Launch Radar — Round 5 Design (`radar-5.0`)

## 1. Concept

**radar-5.0** replaces round 4's hardcoded scale damping and coarse arithmetic peer medians with an empirically calibrated, self-validating comparables engine built strictly from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot's own figures. Three concrete, snapshot-derived upgrades drive a measurable leap over `radar-4.1`: first, **within-sector empirical TVL scale elasticity paired with size-normalized log-space hierarchical peer calibration across seven economic sectors** (splitting Spot DEX & Liquidity from Derivatives & Basis and splitting RWA & Credit from Chains & Infra, fitting the log-TVL compression slope directly from the 101 eligible live DeFi peers rather than hardcoding a near-flat exponent, normalizing every live peer to a $100M TVL anchor before geometric Empirical-Bayes shrinkage, and deriving per-sector Bear/Base/Bull interquartile valuation bands from live peer log-residuals); second, **smooth saturating momentum and Michaelis-Menten token-less duration curves** (replacing piecewise linear growth kinks that over-rewarded single-week +4,448% launch spikes with a smooth inverse-hyperbolic-sine momentum curve, a saturating survival curve for days without a token, and log-diminishing multi-chain credit); and third, **snapshot-derived sibling-product hygiene plus built-in Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)** that automatically removes hyphenated sub-products of already-trading protocol families from the early radar while backtesting the entire valuation engine out-of-sample on all 101 live peers (empirically reducing mean absolute log error from 1.671 to 1.565 and lifting within-2x valuation accuracy from 18.8% to 28.7%). Combined with a freshly architected modular dashboard that displays live comparable peers, empirical valuation bands, and all six concrete farm steps cleanly above the fold in the detail drawer, `radar-5.0` directly widens our margin across all five judge criteria in priority order.

## 2. What Changes vs Round 4

### Model Changes
- **Empirical TVL scale elasticity**: Replaces round 4's hardcoded `-0.06` scale exponent (which overvalued billion-dollar pre-token protocols above live multi-billion-dollar peers like Lido and EigenCloud) with a within-sector demeaned log-log regression slope estimated directly from the snapshot's eligible live peers, shrunk toward `-0.20` and clamped to `[-0.30, -0.08]` (fitting to `-0.3000` on the sealed snapshot, with scale multiplier bounded to `[0.35, 1.75]` around a `$100M` anchor).
- **Size-normalized log-space peer calibration**: Strips out protocol-size confounding before aggregating live peers by normalizing every peer's multiple to the `$100M` TVL anchor in log space, then applying 15% symmetric trimmed means and Empirical-Bayes shrinkage in log space rather than arithmetic ratio space (preventing high-multiple outliers from skewing sector priors or slamming into an artificial `0.72x` ceiling).
- **Seven granular economic sectors**: Expands round 4's five sectors to seven cleanly separated economic families—splitting Spot DEX & Liquidity from Derivatives & Basis, splitting RWA & Credit from Chains & Infra, re-housing Collateral Markets under Staking & Security, and keeping Governance Incentives out of Liquid Staking's low-multiple drag.
- **Smooth saturating growth, duration, and chain curves**: Replaces round 4's kinked piecewise-linear growth multiplier with an inverse-hyperbolic-sine curve that smoothly tamps down extreme outliers (such as `+4,448.5%` 7-day spikes); replaces the 4-year log duration ramp with a Michaelis-Menten battle-testing curve (half-life 240 days); and replaces linear chain steps with log-diminishing chain credit.
- **Empirical Bear / Base / Bull valuation bands & LOOCV scorecard**: Computes each sector's 25th- and 75th-percentile size-adjusted live-peer residual spread to publish a data-driven interquartile valuation range per protocol, plus an out-of-sample Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation benchmark across all 101 live peers stored in the radar payload.

### Board Changes (Row Count & Coverage)
- **Watchlist coverage**: Retains strict 100% coverage of all 40 watchlist slugs (`>= $50M` TVL, no token recorded).
- **Sibling-product early-radar hygiene**: Adds a snapshot-derived separation filter that excludes non-watchlist early-radar candidates whose hyphenated leading slug root (at least 4 characters before the first hyphen) matches the leading slug root of an already-trading protocol in the snapshot—cleanly removing four live-token family sub-products (`flying-tulip-ftusd`, `kava-mint`, `manta-cedefi`, `metalx-swap`) that round 4 inadvertently ranked.
- **Board size**: Moves from 178 rows (40 watchlist + 138 early) to **174 strictly pre-token rows** (40 watchlist + 134 clean early-radar protocols), comfortably inside the 100–4,000 gate range and strictly cleaner on pre-token vs live-token separation.
- **Live peers dataset**: Expands the separated Live Peers view from 60 capped rows to all **101 calibrated live DeFi peers** (each carrying its actual market cap, LOOCV predicted market cap, and actual Mcap/TVL ratio) alongside summary accounting of all 416 already-trading protocols and 4 filtered sibling products set aside from the board.

### Farm-Plan Changes
- **Six ultra-concrete, zero-clipping steps**: Streamlines round 4's 7 steps into **6 tightly engineered steps per row** (each 140–340 characters, strictly inside the 20–600 character gate bound) so that every single step is simultaneously visible above the fold in the 1440×1040 detail drawer screenshot with zero cutoff.
- **Accurate native chain gas table**: Replaces generic `"ETH/native gas"` and fallback `"native gas"` labels with an explicit gas profile table covering the top 25+ chains present in the snapshot (naming exact native tokens such as ETH on Base/Arbitrum/Optimism/Linea/Scroll/Unichain/Katana/Ink/Abstract, MON on Monad, POL on Polygon, AVAX on Avalanche, APT on Aptos, BERA on Berachain, S on Sonic, FLR on Flare, PLUME on Plume Mainnet, XPL on Plasma, HYPE/USDC on Hyperliquid L1, TAO on Bittensor, SUI on Sui, SOL on Solana, BNB on Binance, XLM on Stellar, TRX on Tron, BTC on Bitcoin).
- **Economic rationality metric**: Adds a computed **Gas-to-Capital Drag (%)** indicator alongside Target Capital, Est. Total Gas, Hold Horizon, and Active Chain so a farmer immediately sees fee efficiency.

### Dashboard Changes
- **Fresh modular architecture & distinct visual identity**: Rebuilt from scratch in four clean dashboard files under a bespoke **Warm Ivory, Deep Obsidian, Forest Emerald & Burnished Copper** financial-intelligence aesthetic (replacing round 4's teal-on-navy look).
- **Higher above-the-fold density on the board**: Tightens the masthead and overview strip so **8–9 ranked protocols** appear cleanly above the fold on the main board screenshot alongside a custom zero-dependency **SVG Empirical Log-TVL vs Mcap/TVL Calibration Plot** paired with the Top-8 Valuations chart.
- **Richer filtering & sorting**: Adds a dedicated **Sector** filter and a **Low-Gas Chain** quick toggle alongside Search, Cohort tabs, Category, Chain, and 7d Momentum, plus sortable columns including Implied Value (with inline Bear–Bull range), TVL, 7d TVL, No-Token Age, Peer Multiple, and Farm Gas.
- **Upgraded Detail Drawer**: Adds the **Empirical Bear / Base / Bull IQR Band** bar and a **Top-3 Nearest Live Peer Comps** mini-table (showing actual live market caps and multiples of peers in the same category/sector) above the 2-column Valuation Waterfall, Exact Parity Badge, 5-stat Farm Summary Strip, and all 6 Farm Steps visible in one viewport.
- **Model & Validation Tab**: Upgrades round 4's Model & Sandbox tab with an explicit **LOOCV Live-Peer Validation Scorecard** (within-2x accuracy, within-3x accuracy, mean/median log error, Spearman rank correlation) alongside the interactive 3-slider sensitivity sandbox and 7-sector calibration table.

### What Stays
- Exact contract compliance with `RULES.txt` and all seven gate checks (`dashboard_files`, `board_file`, `sorted_by_value`, `coverage`, `farm_steps`, `screenshots`, `dossier_matches`).

## 4. Key Logic (Prose Only)

### `lib/types.ts`
Defines strongly typed structures for the sealed ten-field snapshot protocol object, the root snapshot container, the `radar-5.0` configuration object (version label `"radar-5.0"`, anchor TVL of one hundred million dollars, peer ratio clip bounds of 0.015 and 2.50, 15% symmetric trim fraction, elasticity prior of -0.20 with prior weight 20 and clamp range -0.30 to -0.08, sector shrinkage prior weight 5, category prior weight 4 for three or more peers and 7 for exactly two peers, peer multiple clamp bounds of 0.05 and 0.85, scale multiplier clamp bounds of 0.35 and 1.75, growth clamp range of -75% to +250% with inverse-hyperbolic-sine scale 20, sensitivity 0.14, clamp range 0.65 to 1.55, and null prior 0.96, Michaelis-Menten duration base 0.72, span 0.36, half-life 240 days, and null prior 0.88, multi-chain logarithmic step 0.04 capped at 1.12, and Bear/Bull factor clamps of 0.35 to 0.85 and 1.20 to 2.60). Also defines types for sector and category calibration records, LOOCV summary statistics, per-row live comparable references, valuation breakdowns, Bear/Base/Bull ranges, farm summaries, board rows, live peer audit rows, and the dashboard `RadarPayload`.

### `lib/calibration.ts`
First classifies every snapshot protocol into pre-token (all three of market cap, CoinGecko identifier, and symbol are null) versus live-token (at least one of those three is present). A live-token protocol enters the calibration pool when both its market cap and TVL are strictly positive and its category is not CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, or Cross Chain Bridge.

Next maps every category string (lowercased, defaulting empty when null) deterministically into one of seven economic sectors in strict priority order: first, Spot DEX & Liquidity for categories matching DEX, Dexs, or liquidity manager; second, Derivatives & Basis for categories matching derivative, perpetual, option, basis, or prediction; third, Lending & Credit for categories containing lending, CDP, or stablecoin; fourth, Asset Mgmt & Yield for categories containing curator, allocator, yield, farm, farming, indexes, or CeDeFi; fifth, Staking & Security for categories containing staking, restaking, restaked, BTC, or collateral; sixth, RWA & Credit for categories matching the whole word RWA; and seventh, Chains & Infra for all remaining categories including Chain, Payments, Privacy, AI Agents, Decentralized AI, Token Locker, NFT Marketplace, Launchpad, Insurance, Governance Incentives, Crypto Card Issuer, DOR, or uncategorized.

Then estimates the snapshot's empirical TVL scale elasticity directly from the live calibration pool. For each eligible live peer, compute log relative TVL as the natural logarithm of its TVL divided by one hundred million dollars, and compute its clipped log multiple as the natural logarithm of its market-cap-to-TVL ratio clamped between 0.015 and 2.50. Group peers by their seven economic sectors, compute each sector's mean log relative TVL and mean clipped log multiple, and subtract those sector means from each peer's values so sector-level size differences do not confound the slope. Compute the pooled within-sector ordinary least squares slope as the sum of demeaned cross-products divided by the sum of squared demeaned log relative TVLs (falling back to -0.20 if the denominator is zero). Shrink that raw slope toward -0.20 by multiplying the raw slope by the live peer count, adding twenty times -0.20, and dividing by the live peer count plus twenty; clamp the result to the interval -0.30 through -0.08 and round to four decimal places.

Next normalizes every eligible live peer's valuation multiple to the one-hundred-million-dollar TVL anchor in log space by subtracting the product of the fitted scale elasticity and that peer's log relative TVL from its clipped log multiple. Compute the global anchor log multiple using a 15% symmetric trimmed mean across all eligible peers (sort ascending, drop the integer floor of fifteen percent of the count from each end whenever at least five observations exist, and average the remaining core). For each of the seven sectors, compute the raw 15% trimmed mean of its peers' size-normalized log multiples (or the global log multiple if empty), then shrink toward the global log multiple using prior weight 5 by taking the weighted average of the sector count times its raw trimmed mean plus five times the global log multiple, divided by the sector count plus five. For every category present in the snapshot, compute its shrunk log multiple against its parent sector's shrunk log multiple: when a category has three or more live peers, shrink its 15% trimmed mean toward the sector log multiple with prior weight 4; when it has exactly two live peers, shrink its mean toward the sector log multiple with prior weight 7; when it has zero or one live peer, inherit the parent sector's shrunk log multiple directly so single-peer anomalies cannot distort valuations. Exponentiate each category's shrunk log multiple, clamp it between 0.05 and 0.85, and round to four decimal places to obtain the category's final peer multiple.

To construct empirical Bear and Bull valuation bands, compute each eligible live peer's size-adjusted log residual by subtracting its parent sector's shrunk log multiple from its size-normalized log multiple. For each sector with at least five live peers, take the 25th and 75th percentiles (linearly interpolated on the sorted residuals) of that sector's residuals; for smaller sectors, fall back to the global 25th and 75th percentiles across all eligible peers. Exponentiate those two quartile residuals, clamp the Bear factor to 0.35 through 0.85 and the Bull factor to 1.20 through 2.60, and round both to four decimal places.

Also select up to three nearest live-token comparable peers for any target protocol by prioritizing eligible live peers in the exact same category (falling back to the same economic sector when fewer than three exist), ranked by smallest absolute difference in log TVL with ascending slug as deterministic tie-breaker, recording each comparable's name, symbol, category, TVL, market cap, and actual market-cap-to-TVL ratio.

Finally, run full Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation across all eligible live peers: for each peer in turn, exclude that peer from the calibration pool, refit the entire within-sector elasticity and hierarchical log-space calibration from the remaining peers alone, score the held-out peer using the exact valuation engine, and record the predicted market cap. Aggregate across all held-out peers to compute mean absolute log error, median absolute log error, share of predictions within a factor of two of actual market cap, share within a factor of three, and Spearman rank correlation between out-of-sample predicted and actual market caps.

### `lib/valuation.ts`
Computes the exact implied launch value and full audit breakdown for a single protocol using five multipliers, each rounded to four decimal places before multiplication:

1. **Size-Normalized Peer Multiple**: Look up the protocol's calibrated category peer multiple from the calibration object (falling back to the clamped global anchor multiple if ever absent), alongside a human-readable peer source label naming whether it came from a full category, thin category, or sector prior and the exact peer count.
2. **Empirical TVL Scale Elasticity**: Divide the protocol's TVL (floored at one dollar) by one hundred million dollars, raise that ratio to the power of the snapshot-fitted scale elasticity, clamp the result between 0.35 and 1.75, and round to four decimal places.
3. **Smooth Saturating 7-Day Growth Momentum**: If seven-day TVL change is null or not finite, set the multiplier directly to the conservative prior 0.9600. Otherwise clamp the percentage change to the interval -75 through +250, divide by 20, take the inverse hyperbolic sine, multiply by 0.14, take the natural exponential, clamp to the interval 0.65 through 1.55, and round to four decimal places.
4. **Michaelis-Menten Token-less Duration**: Compute exact whole days between the snapshot date and the protocol's listing date at UTC midnight, floored at zero (returning null if the listing date is null or invalid). When null, set the multiplier directly to the conservative prior 0.8800. Otherwise compute 0.72 plus 0.36 times the ratio of elapsed days to elapsed days plus 240, and round to four decimal places.
5. **Log-Diminishing Multi-Chain Footprint**: Take the natural logarithm of the protocol's chain count (floored at one), multiply by 0.04, add one, clamp to the interval 1.00 through 1.12, and round to four decimal places.

Multiply the protocol's whole-dollar TVL by all five four-decimal rounded multipliers in order, round to the nearest whole dollar, and floor at one dollar to produce `implied_value_usd`. Multiply `implied_value_usd` by the sector's four-decimal Bear factor and Bull factor (each rounded to whole dollars, floored at one) to obtain the empirical interquartile Bear and Bull bounds. Provide an independent verification helper that recomputes every row's implied value strictly from its raw TVL and rounded multipliers and reports any single-dollar discrepancy.

### `lib/farm-planner.ts`
Generates a concrete, non-generic 6-step farm plan and 5-metric summary strip for every protocol strictly from its snapshot chains, category, TVL, token-less duration, and 7-day momentum. Maintains a deterministic chain gas catalog covering Ethereum, Solana, Bitcoin, Binance, Tron, Sui, Aptos, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Monad, Hyperliquid L1, Plasma, Avalanche, Unichain, Robinhood Chain, Berachain, Polygon, Sonic, Flare, Plume Mainnet, Katana, Linea, Scroll, Ink, Abstract, Arc, Stellar, Bittensor, Hemi, TAC, zkSync Era, Mantle, Noble, Algorand, Ripple, Mezo, BSquared, Babylon Genesis, and a conservative fallback—specifying each chain's exact native gas token, single-transaction USD cost, recommended gas buffer, low-fee boolean flag, and execution note.

Selects the protocol's optimal active chain by preferring the first low-fee chain in its deployment list (falling back to the first listed chain when all chains are high-fee L1s) and selects a distinct low-fee secondary chain when multi-chain. Assigns capital sizing across five TVL tiers (500 dollars for flagship protocols at or above 500 million TVL; 250 dollars for core watchlist at 100–500 million; 150 dollars for mid-cap watchlist at 50–100 million, raised to 250 dollars on high-gas L1-only deployments; 100 dollars for early radar at 15–50 million, raised to 200 dollars on L1-only; and 50 dollars risk-capped for emerging protocols under 15 million, raised to 150 dollars on L1-only). Determines the qualifying hold horizon (60 days when 7-day TVL momentum is at or above +10% or token-less duration is under 180 days; 45 days when duration is unknown or between 180 and 450 days; 30 days for established protocols over 450 days). Branches core capital deployment, secondary multiplier leg, recurring cadence, and clean exit instructions across twelve DeFi category mechanics (Risk Curators, Capital Allocators, DEXs & Liquidity Managers, Lending & Collateral, CDPs & Stablecoins, Derivatives/Options/Basis, Prediction Markets, RWAs, Staking & Restaking, Yield/CeDeFi/Indexes, Chains/Payments/Cards, and general DeFi). Sums every gas leg (wallet funding, entry, optional secondary leg, weekly or monthly recurring touchpoints, and exit/revoke) to compute exact total estimated gas and gas-to-capital drag percentage, then formats six structured step strings strictly within 20–600 characters.

### `lib/board-builder.ts`
Parses and strictly validates `snapshot.json` (date format, source string, positive integer floor, ten-field protocol array, string watchlist). Builds the ranked pre-token universe by taking all 40 watchlist slugs unconditionally as the floor, then adding every pre-token protocol below the watchlist floor that passes clean early-radar hygiene: not an excluded wrapped/bridge/staking/CEX category, no version suffix in the name, no whole word wrapped/staked/staking, no major exchange prefix, AND no hyphenated leading slug root (four or more characters before the first hyphen) matching the leading slug root of any live-token protocol in the snapshot. Scores all selected protocols using `lib/valuation.ts`, attaches their 6-step farm plans from `lib/farm-planner.ts` and top-3 live peer comps from `lib/calibration.ts`, sorts strictly by descending `implied_value_usd` with ascending `slug` tie-breaker, and assigns sequential ranks 1 through N. Builds the separated Live Peers audit array covering all 101 calibrated live DeFi peers (with their LOOCV out-of-sample predictions) plus total counts of all live-token and filtered sibling protocols. Validates the complete board against every gate invariant (row count 100–4,000, valid unique slugs, 100% watchlist coverage, exact ranks 1..N, non-increasing implied values, non-constant values, 3–12 farm steps of 20–600 trimmed characters) and serializes `window.RADAR` cleanly for `data.js`.

## 5. Farm Plans

Every protocol row emits exactly **6 concrete, tailored farm steps** into `board.json` and `data.js`, formatted consistently as:

`N. Step Title: Concrete protocol-, chain-, and category-specific action. Cost: Explicit USD capital, gas, and fee figure. Timeframe: Explicit minutes, weekly cadence, or hold duration.`

Every trimmed step targets **150–320 characters** (comfortably inside the gate's 20–600 character requirement and compact enough that all 6 steps render cleanly inside a single 1440×1040 detail drawer screenshot).

### The 6-Step Template & Dynamic Tailoring Rules
1. **Isolated wallet & chain gas buffer** — Names the protocol's selected low-fee active chain (or L1 chain if L1-only), exact native gas token (e.g., ETH on Base/Arbitrum, MON on Monad, SOL on Solana, BNB on Binance, TAO on Bittensor, XPL on Plasma), dollar buffer, and chain-specific execution guidance (such as off-peak below-10-gwei batching on Ethereum or rent reserve on Solana). Cost states exact funding gas plus buffer USD; Timeframe states 10 minutes.
2. **Core capital deployment** — Tailored across twelve DeFi category playbooks (Risk Curators deposit into highest-liquidity curated vault; Capital Allocators mint core receipt shares; DEXs split 70% into deep stable/major LP and 30% into two organic swaps; Lending supplies collateral and enables collateral mode; CDPs mint at >=180% collateral ratio; Derivatives/Basis opens conservative <=2x maker or delta-neutral margin; Prediction Markets places hedged liquid-market positions; RWAs completes eligibility and mints yield-bearing RWA token; Staking/Restaking delegates to high-uptime operator; Yield/CeDeFi deposits into audited flagship vault; Chains/Payments bridges via canonical ramp and runs native transfers; general DeFi commits into core audited contract). Capital scales strictly by TVL dilution tier ($500 flagship `>=$500M`, $250 core watchlist `$100M–$500M`, $150 mid-cap watchlist `$50M–$100M`, $100 early radar `$15M–$50M`, $50 emerging `<$15M`, with L1-only floors raised to $250/$200/$150 so gas drag stays rational). Cost states exact capital USD plus entry gas; Timeframe states 15 minutes.
3. **Secondary multiplier or multi-chain leg** — Dynamically branches on whether a low-fee secondary chain exists and on protocol category: when multi-chain, routes a $25–$30 satellite deposit/swap on the secondary chain (naming its exact chain and gas cost); when single-chain, executes the category's native second track (35% LTV correlated borrow for Lending, stability-pool staking for CDPs, hedged maker volume cycle for Derivatives, range tightening or fee harvest for DEXs, or pure non-splitting wallet retention for Curators/RWAs/Staking with $0 extra gas). Cost states exact secondary gas and any side capital or swap/borrow fee; Timeframe states 5–15 minutes.
4. **Recurring activity cadence** — Adapts frequency to chain gas economics and category: low-fee chains run weekly or bi-weekly on-chain touchpoints (10 minutes/week across the hold weeks); Ethereum/Bitcoin L1-only protocols switch to off-chain weekly monitoring plus one batched off-peak monthly touchpoint so gas never erodes principal; pure RWA holding avoids unnecessary redemption fees ($0 recurring gas). Cost states per-tx gas and cumulative recurring gas over the hold window; Timeframe states weekly minutes and total weeks.
5. **Qualifying hold & liquidity risk guard** — Sets exact continuous hold duration from token-less age and 7-day momentum (60 days for surging `>=+10%` or young `<180d` protocols; 45 days for mid-age `180–450d` or unlisted; 30 days for established `>450d`). Appends an explicit liquidity-depth warning whenever 7-day TVL dropped by 10% or more, or otherwise states the TVL-tier dilution posture.
